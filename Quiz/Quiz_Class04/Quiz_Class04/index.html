    Quiz Class 04 - Data Wrangling - Basic Manipulation¶

    Q1¶

    Question:

    In the ASX dataset, what does each row represent?

    Options:

    • A. A single firm's annual audited financial statement data for the reporting year 2024.
    • B. The daily share price movements for a large ASX-listed firm
    • C. A monthly summary of a firm's sales and expenses
    • D. The combined financial results of all firms in a given industry

    Answer: A

    Q2¶

    Question:

    Which of the following tools (functions) are included in the dplyr package for data manipulation in R?

    Options:

    • A. lm(), predict(), anova(), confint()
    • B. read_csv(), write_csv(), read_excel(), write_excel()
    • C. filter(), mutate(), select(), arrange(), summarise(), group_by()
    • D. ggplot(), geom_point(), facet_wrap(), scale_color_manual()

    Answer: C

    Q3¶

    Question:

    What does the filter() function in dplyr do?

    Options:

    • A. Changes the order of rows in a data frame
    • B. Removes specific columns from a data frame
    • C. Keeps rows based on conditions applied to column values
    • D. Creates new variables in a data frame

    Answer: C

    Q4¶

    Question:

    What does the arrange() function in dplyr do?

    Options:

    • A. Changes the order of rows in a data frame based on column values
    • B. Keeps rows based on conditions
    • C. Changes which columns are present in the data frame
    • D. Groups data for aggregation

    Answer: A

    Q5¶

    Question:

    Based on the plot of the top ten ASX-listed companies by EBIT in 2024, which of the following statements is most accurate?

    Options:

    • A. Mining companies make up the majority of the top ten and have higher combined profits than non-mining companies.
    • B. Non-mining companies outnumber mining companies in the top ten and have higher combined profits.
    • C. Mining companies are fewer in number but have higher combined profits than the non-mining companies in the top ten.
    • D. Non-mining companies dominate both in number and in total profits within the top ten.

    Answer: C
